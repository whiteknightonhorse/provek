# ADR-0004. The incubator's own passport shows ITS OWN gaps

**Date:** 2026-08-19. **Status:** accepted.

## Decision
The incubator ran its own procedure on itself and published a passport in which: the binding is
marked WEAK (`git`, revocable); one operation is measured; two are `unreadable`, not L0; coverage
is named (github inspected, treasury and server out of reach); and the missing deployed artefact
is recorded as "the comparison was not performed, and that is not a violation by the subject".

## Why this is the right result rather than a weak one
The temptation was to award ourselves a high score - our methodology, our tree. Instead the
passport shows that the incubator has proved LITTLE about itself. That is exactly how the product
must behave towards others: a projection built from one measured operation is an honest "we
measured one of three", not "60% autonomous".

## What it proves
ABI-31-4 is closed by deed: same protocol, same schema, same publication. A verifier that exempts
itself would have published an opinion.
